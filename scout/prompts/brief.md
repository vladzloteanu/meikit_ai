# Founder brief for idea generation

Shareable with outside models: it holds skills and constraints only. Nothing from `founders/`,
no employer name, no incident, no client, no person.

## Who

- Two founders. The lead is a senior software and product executive based in France: twenty years
  from backend engineering to a CTO-level role, still employed full time. The side business gets a
  few hours a week. A cofounder joins; their profile comes later.
- His edges:
  - buying traffic profitably on Google, Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn: cost per lead, funnels,
    conversion tracking;
  - building ad-intelligence tools: public ad libraries, reach tracking over time;
  - running AI agents in production: scheduled agents, tool use, human checks, cost control;
  - knowing French small-business admin from the inside: the e-invoicing reform, domiciliation,
    freelancer copyright contracts and payment notices, furnished-rental (LMNP) accounting,
    supplier invoices missing from expense tools;
  - affiliate marketing;
  - fluent French, English and Romanian, with access to low-cost freelancers in Romania.

## Constraints

- **No competing with the day job.** Nothing that competes with lead-generation companies or
  performance-marketing agencies, and nothing sold to advertisers who buy leads. No consumer-finance
  or dating lead generation.
- **No heavy operations.** No enterprise sales cycles, no product that needs 24/7 on-call, no
  regulated advice (legal, medical, financial), no two-sided marketplace, no physical goods, no
  weekly content treadmill.
- **The target.** At least 10k EUR a month of net revenue within 12–24 months. That is about 14k
  EUR of monthly revenue, so 100 customers or fewer at 150–1,000 EUR a month (B2B or prosumer),
  self-serve or one call to sign. Or a productized service delivered mostly by agents with a human
  check.
- **Where.** France and the EU first (the language and compliance edge); the US is allowed.
- **Money first.** Prefer jobs people already pay for: a competitor, a freelancer, staff time, a
  mandatory deadline.

## Facts from our research (October 2026; sources in our report)

- **Forced changes with thin vendor supply:**
  - EU Cyber Resilience Act vulnerability reporting for small manufacturers (since 2026-09-11);
  - the EU ban on generic green claims (since 2026-09-27; France has not transposed it);
  - TLS certificates capped at 100 days (from 2027-03-15), for devices that cannot renew
    themselves;
  - EU packaging regulation conformity files (applies from August 2026);
  - the French solar-canopy obligation for car parks of 1,500–10,000 m² (by 2028-07-01);
  - EUDR due diligence for micro and small importers (by 2027-06-30).

  French e-invoicing (SMEs must issue from 2027-09-01) is crowded: 130–156 approved platforms,
  some free.
- **Where money is visible:**
  - marketplaces: the Atlassian Marketplace (sortable by top-grossing), WordPress.org, the Chrome
    Web Store, Shopify (installs through StoreCensus);
  - verified revenue: TrustMRR (15,000+ startups), Flippa and Empire Flippers listings;
  - paid labour: Codeur.com and Freelancer.com project budgets.
- **Base rates:**
  - products under $50 a month churn badly: AI products there keep about 23 % of revenue;
  - higher entry prices grow faster;
  - full-time founders grow about 30 % faster than part-time ones.
- **French business creations:** a record 1,165,800 in 2025. Q3 2026 is up 16 % year on year,
  with information and communication up 55 %. Admin support, cleaning and specialised e-commerce
  are also growing.
- **Recent r/smallbusiness pains (Oct 2026):**
  - form-builder prices rising;
  - client conversations about money held on WhatsApp;
  - quotes and payments;
  - a small bakery where things "slip through the cracks";
  - replying to reviews at scale;
  - shared email.
