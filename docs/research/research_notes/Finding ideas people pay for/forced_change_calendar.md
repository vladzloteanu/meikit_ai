## 1. France: the e-invoicing reform and the other dated obligations that hit SMBs in 2026–2028

### Takeaway
E-invoicing is the only French change in the window that forces nearly every company to act.
- Receiving e-invoices has been mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses since 1 September 2026.
- Issuing e-invoices and e-reporting become mandatory for SMEs and micro-businesses on 1 September 2027.
- Attempts to postpone it failed. The approved-platform market is saturated: 130 to 156 platforms, with
  free tiers.

The other dated French obligations are narrower:
- tertiary buildings of 1,000 m² or more;
- car parks of 1,500 m² or more;
- F-rated rentals from 2028;
- companies using more than 2.75 GWh of energy a year.

Several obligations are slipping:
- pay transparency, now expected 2027–2028;
- NIS2, whose law is still not adopted;
- the end of paper meal vouchers, moved to 2028 and awaiting the Senate;
- the anti-greenwashing (ECGT) and right-to-repair directives, which France has not transposed.

#### 1.1 E-invoicing and e-reporting (facturation électronique) [LAW]
- **What changes.**
  - B2B invoices between French VAT-registered companies must be exchanged as structured
    e-invoices through an approved platform (*plateforme agréée*, PA).
  - The public portal (PPF) no longer handles invoices, so approved platforms are the only route.
  - Chorus Pro stays the channel for invoices to public bodies and is registered as a public platform
    from 2026-09-01 ([Guidefacturation](https://guidefacturation.com/reforme-facturation-electronique/);
    [KPMG Avocats, 2024](https://kpmg.com/av/fr/avocats/eclairages/2024/10/facturation-electronique-le-schema-initialement-prevu-est-modifie.html)).
- **Dates.**
  - Receiving is mandatory for every company from 2026-09-01, "whatever their size or legal form".
  - Issuing and e-reporting are mandatory for large firms and ETIs (mid-sized firms) from 2026-09-01,
    and for SMEs, micro-businesses and micro-enterprises from 2027-09-01.
  - Timing depends on company size only, not on activity.
  - The receiving duty is triggered in practice only when the supplier must issue electronically
    ([ITSocial, 2026-08-12](https://itsocial.fr/metiers/metiers-actualites/facturation-electronique-la-dgfip-annonce-une-tolerance-au-demarrage-et-ouvre-un-numero-dassistance/)).
  - The government's Service-Public page gives the same 2026/2027 split ("vérifié le 11 août 2026")
    ([Service-Public F31808](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31808)) (off.).
- **New mandatory invoice mentions:**
  - the client's SIREN (company ID number);
  - the delivery address if it differs from the billing address;
  - the nature of the operation (goods, services or both);
  - the "option pour le paiement de la taxe d'après les débits" (VAT due on invoicing), where chosen.

  Source: [Service-Public F31808](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31808) (off.).
- **Who and how many.**
  - A parliamentary written question puts close to **10 million** VAT-registered economic actors in
    scope of the 2026-09-01 receiving duty
    ([AN written question 18851](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q17/17-18851QE.htm)) (off.).
  - Amendment texts cite **3.7 million** small local businesses (*entreprises de proximité*)
    ([AN amendment 149](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/amendements/0481/CSVIEECO/149.pdf)) (off.).
  - By August 2026 about **2 million** companies had declared a receiving platform. This is a
    self-declaration, not verified capacity
    ([ITSocial](https://itsocial.fr/metiers/metiers-actualites/facturation-electronique-la-dgfip-annonce-une-tolerance-au-demarrage-et-ouvre-un-numero-dassistance/)).
- **Penalties (art. 1737 of the tax code as rewritten by the finance law of 2026-02-19).**
  - Receiving: after a formal notice, the company has 3 months to comply. Then the fine is €500, then
    €1,000 for each further quarter of non-compliance.
  - Issuing: €50 per invoice, capped at €15,000 per year.
  - Error or omission on an invoice: €15.
  - Platform failing its transmission duties: €50 per invoice, capped at €45,000 per year.
  - A first breach is forgiven if corrected within 30 days.
  - Source: [ITSocial](https://itsocial.fr/metiers/metiers-actualites/facturation-electronique-la-dgfip-annonce-une-tolerance-au-demarrage-et-ouvre-un-numero-dassistance/).
  - Conflict on e-reporting:
    - [DAF Mag](https://www.daf-mag.fr/reglementation-1243/fiscalite-2115/facturation-electronique-le-plf-2026-renforce-les-sanctions-pour-les-entreprises-22974)
      and [WeInvoice](https://weinvoice.fr/blog/sanctions-facture-electronique-2026) give €500 per
      missing e-reporting transmission.
    - [factureelectronique.info](https://factureelectronique.info/blog/sanctions-dgfip-modalites-application/)
      still gives the older €15 per invoice and €250 per transmission.
    - One summary says the cap for platforms rises to €100,000
      ([KPMG Avocats, 2026-03](https://kpmg.com/av/fr/avocats/eclairages/2026/03/facturation-electronique-amenagement-des-obligations-et-renforcement-des-sanctions.html)),
      against €45,000 at ITSocial.
- **Launch tolerance.**
  - Minister David Amiel announced on 10 July 2026 that companies acting in good faith and facing an
    "obstacle attesté" (proven obstacle) will not be sanctioned at the start.
  - This is a policy on how the law is applied, not a change in the law.
  - DGFiP (the tax administration) assistance line: 0806 807 807
    ([ITSocial](https://itsocial.fr/metiers/metiers-actualites/facturation-electronique-la-dgfip-annonce-une-tolerance-au-demarrage-et-ouvre-un-numero-dassistance/)).
  - One law blog says the tolerance runs to 2026-12-31
    ([Cyril Jarnias](https://www.cyriljarnias.fr/facturation-electronique-soutien-entreprises-bonne-foi/)).
  - A late-August article reports that the government ruled out penalties before end-2026
    ([Siècle Digital, 2026-08-27](https://siecledigital.fr/2026/08/27/facturation-electronique-face-aux-craintes-de-piratage-le-gouvernement-veut-rassurer/)).
  - The DGFiP published a start-up guide
    ([DGFiP guide, PDF](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/media/1_metier/2_professionnel/EV/2_gestion/290_facturation_electronique/guide_pratique_facturation_electronique.pdf)) (off.).
- **Postponement attempts failed.**
  - A 2025 attempt in the Assemblée to push issuing to 2027–2028 was voted down
    ([Obat](https://www.obat.fr/blog/facture-electronique/)).
  - An amendment proposing 2028-09-01 for SME and micro issuing lapsed
    ([AN amendment 105](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/amendements/0481/CSVIEECO/105.pdf)) (off.).
- **Market state.**
  - First official list: **101** approved platforms on 2026-01-16
    ([economie.gouv.fr](https://economie.gouv.fr/actualites/facturation-electronique-la-liste-des-101-premieres-plateformes-agreees-est-disponible)) (off.).
  - **130** on the official register by mid-August
    ([ITSocial](https://itsocial.fr/metiers/metiers-actualites/facturation-electronique-la-dgfip-annonce-une-tolerance-au-demarrage-et-ouvre-un-numero-dassistance/)).
  - **149**, plus 14 being finalised, on launch day
    ([Bourse Inside, 2026-09-01](https://bourseinside.fr/economie/facturation-electronique-au-1er-septembre-cette-obligation-change-tout-pour-votre-entreprise-voici-les-149-plateformes-agreees/)).
  - 150 according to [les-experts-comptables.fr](https://les-experts-comptables.fr/ressources/liste-plateformes-agreees-facturation-electronique),
    and 156 tracked by [ma-facture-electronique.org](https://ma-facture-electronique.org/logiciels/logiciel-caisse-nf-525/).
  - Free offers exist, for example
    [Tiime "facturation électronique gratuite"](https://www.tiime.fr/facturation-electronique).

#### 1.2 Pay transparency (transposition of Directive 2023/970) [PENDING]
- France missed the EU deadline of 2026-06-07. The bill went to the Council of Ministers on 2026-09-10.
  The Labour Ministry says Parliament will examine it "only in 2027"
  ([Open Lefebvre Dalloz, 2026-10-07](https://open.lefebvre-dalloz.fr/actualites/droit-social/projet-loi-transparence-salariale-on_f10e356a1-6c2e-4ded-bc41-8836918e6c92)).
- **Content** (same source):
  - Job ads must show a pay range.
  - Employers may not ask candidates about current or past pay, and pay-secrecy clauses are banned.
  - In companies with 50+ staff, employees may ask for average pay by sex in their category, with an
    answer due within 2 months. Breaches cost a €450 fine.
  - Seven indicators replace the gender-equality index (index égalité). The first six are
    pre-calculated from DSN payroll data.
  - The seventh indicator (pay gap by worker category) is phased in by size, as a maximum delay after
    the law is promulgated: 1 year for 150+ staff, 3 years for 100–149, 6 years for 50–99.
- **Estimated effective dates** (the article marks them with question marks): job-ad rules in H2 2027,
  and the right to information and the first indicators in H2 2028.

#### 1.3 Meal vouchers (titres-restaurant) [PENDING]
- **Bill.** The bill to modernise and rebalance meal vouchers ("PPL visant à moderniser et rééquilibrer
  le fonctionnement du titre-restaurant") was adopted at first reading by the Assemblée nationale on
  2026-10-12. The Senate must now examine it.
- **What it would change:**
  - no new paper vouchers from 2028;
  - grocery use extended through 2027, then made permanent from 2028;
  - use on Sundays and public holidays from 2028;
  - 3-year validity from 2028;
  - a cap on issuer commissions set by decree.

  Source: [LégiSocial, 2026-10-20](https://www.legisocial.fr/actualites-sociales/8196-tickets-restaurant-changements-prevus-proposition-loi.html).
- **If no law passes,** the grocery-use exemption lapses on 2026-12-31
  ([GHR via Tendance Hôtellerie](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/25887-article/ghr-titres-restaurant-la-restauration-perd-1-22-milliard-d-euros-de-depenses-par-an-depuis-l-elargissement-aux-courses-alimentaires)).
- **Older dates for the end of paper are obsolete:**
  - Edenred in 2025 gave 2027-01-01
    ([Edenred PR, 2025-06-26](https://www.edenred.com/system/files/documents/cp_reformetr_2025_fr_26062025.pdf)).
  - A July 2025 alert gave 2027-03-01
    ([PSL alert](https://profession-sport-loisirs.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/25-07-2025_ALERTE-321.pdf)).
- **Scale:**
  - About 5.4 million employees, 146,000 client organisations and about 235,000 affiliated merchants
    (CNTR, the meal-voucher commission, via
    [Edenred PR, 2025](https://www.edenred.com/system/files/documents/cp_reformetr_2025_fr_26062025.pdf)).
  - Paper was about 25% of vouchers in 2025
    ([PSL alert](https://profession-sport-loisirs.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/25-07-2025_ALERTE-321.pdf)).
  - More than 5 million beneficiaries and more than 1 billion vouchers a year
    ([CNTR via Tendance Hôtellerie, 2026-07-31](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/19599-article/la-cntr-defend-le-titre-restaurant-un-dispositif-socio-economique-toujours-innovant-pour-plus-de-5-millions-de-francais)).
  - GHR, a restaurant trade body, says restaurants lose €1.22 bn of spending a year to grocery use
    ([Tendance Hôtellerie, 2026-09-21](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/25887-article/ghr-titres-restaurant-la-restauration-perd-1-22-milliard-d-euros-de-depenses-par-an-depuis-l-elargissement-aux-courses-alimentaires)).

#### 1.4 Décret tertiaire / OPERAT [LAW]
- **Annual filing.** Tertiary buildings of 1,000 m² or more must file the year's energy consumption on
  OPERAT, ADEME's platform. The 2025 data were due by 2026-09-30, with no automatic extension
  ([Construires](https://www.construires.fr/operat-declaration-consommations-energie-tertiaire/);
  [Ekwateur](https://ekwateur.fr/blog/marche-de-lenergie-pour-les-pros/declaration-operat/)).
- **Penalties:** up to €7,500 for legal entities, plus public naming (*name and shame*)
  ([Ekwateur](https://ekwateur.fr/blog/marche-de-lenergie-pour-les-pros/declaration-operat/)).
- **Since 2026-07-01:** the energy certificate must be disclosed at sale or lease and displayed on site
  ([Construires](https://www.construires.fr/operat-declaration-consommation-tertiaire/)).
- **Target:** −40% energy use by 2030
  ([Hayot Expertise](https://hayot-expertise.fr/blog/decret-tertiaire-operat-obligations-echeances)).
  Sources disagree on whether the first official check is in 2030 or 2031
  ([Group GAC](https://group-gac.com/en/decree-tertiary-conformance/) vs opera-energie.com).
- **Scale** (secondary summary of ADEME's review):
  - about 1.145 million filings by late January 2026, covering about 340,000 declared units
    (*entités fonctionnelles assujetties*, EFA);
  - an estimated scope of about 1.2 billion m²;
  - a fill rate of 50–60%.

  Source: [Opera Énergie](https://opera-energie.com/media/la-consommation-energetique-du-parc-tertiaire-recule-de-26-pourcent-depuis-2010/).
  The figures do not reconcile internally (see Gaps).

#### 1.5 Building automation (décret BACS) [LAW]
- For existing tertiary buildings with heating or cooling of 70–290 kW, the deadline moved from
  2027-01-01 to **2030-01-01**. Systems above 290 kW stay at 2025-01-01.
- The postponement decree was published in the Journal officiel on 26–27 December 2025. One guide
  numbers it 2025-1343.
- Sources: [Rexel](https://entreprise.rexel.fr/courant-positif/tous-nos-articles/articles/report-du-decret-bacs-une-nouvelle-echeance-fixee-a-2030/);
  [Sobre Énergie](https://www.sobre-energie.com/actus/decret-bacs-le-report-a-2030-est-officiel/);

#### 1.6 Energy-rating (DPE) rental bans [LAW, plus a PENDING housing bill]
- **Schedule.**
  - G-rated homes: no new lease or renewal since 2025-01-01.
  - F-rated: from **2028-01-01**.
  - E-rated: from 2034-01-01.
  - Overseas departments run 3 years later (F in 2031).
  - Sources: [Hagnéré Patrimoine](https://www.hagnere-patrimoine.fr/guides-patrimoine/investissement-immobilier/calendrier-interdictions-location-dpe-2025-2034);
    [Habitatclair](https://habitatclair.fr/guides/logement-social/logement-classe-f-interdiction-de-location-des-2028/).
- **Housing bill (relance logement).**
  - Senate first reading on 2026-07-08. Assemblée committee on 2026-09-21/22, report on 2026-09-23.
    No date set for the full-chamber debate.
  - The bill does **not** move the 2028 and 2034 dates.
  - It would add exemptions. For example, a works contract signed before 2030 would make the home
    count as decent for up to 3 years, or 5 years in a co-owned building. It would also set a cutoff
    for leases in progress.
  - Sources: [Kohen Avocats, 2026-10-07](https://kohenavocats.fr/2026/10/07/location-logement-dpe-f-g-octobre-2026-interdiction-derogations-senat-recours-2026/);
    [France DPE](https://france-dpe.fr/articles/loi-relance-logement-dpe-g-location).
- **Change since 2026-01-01.** The electricity conversion factor in the energy rating fell from 2.3 to
  1.9. The government estimated 600,000–850,000 F/G homes would be reclassified
  ([Bailfacile](https://www.bailfacile.fr/actualites/dpe-2026-declassement-ou-reclassement-de-votre-logement);
  [Human Immobilier](https://www.human-immobilier.fr/immo-news/location/reforme-dpe-2026-reclassement-logements-electriques/1558)).
- **How many.** About 1.4 million F-rated homes in 2025, about 720,000 of them privately rented (CPIM,
  citing ONRE, the national renovation observatory)
  ([CPIM](https://www.cpim.fr/dpe-2028-derniere-ligne-droite/)). This conflicts with about 1.2 million
  F-rated homes in the private rental stock at
  [Cafpi](https://www.cafpi.fr/credit-immobilier/actualites/dpe-f-interdit-a-la-location-en-2026-ce-qui-change-vraiment-pour-les-proprietaires).

#### 1.7 Solar canopies on car parks (loi APER) [LAW]
- **The obligation.** Outdoor car parks of 10,000 m² or more must cover half their area with solar
  canopies by **2026-07-01**. The 1,500–10,000 m² band has until **2028-07-01**
  ([L'Écho du Solaire](https://www.lechodusolaire.fr/ombrieres-de-parkings-la-premiere-echeance-de-la-loi-aper-depassee-la-conformite-reste-tres-faible/);
  [Irisolaris](https://irisolaris.com/loi-aper-obligations-ombrieres-photovoltaiques/)).
- **Fines:** up to €40,000 a year per site of 10,000 m² or more, and €20,000 a year below that
  ([SIRENERGIES](https://www.sirenergies.com/en/article/loi-aper-2026-obligations-entreprises);
  [Tesla Mag](https://www.tesla-mag.com/ombrieres-solaires-obligatoires-ce-que-risquent-les-grands-parkings-depuis-le-1er-juillet/)).
- **Loi Huwart (2025-11-26).**
  - Vegetation may be mixed with the canopies if at least 35% of the shaded half is solar.
  - Postponement to 2028-01-01 requires a deposit-backed contract signed before 2026-06-30 and a panel
    order placed before 2026-12-31.
  - Sources: [Terre Solaire](https://terresolaire.com/Blog/reglementation-solaire/obligation-ombriere-parking/);
    [Ombritech](https://ombritech.com/loi-aper/); [Weblex](https://www.weblex.fr/weblex-actualite/panneaux-photovoltaiques-et-equipement-des-parkings-un-delai-supplementaire).
- **Eligibility decree.** Decree 2026-842 of 2026-09-07 (1src:
  [Terre Solaire](https://terresolaire.com/Blog/reglementation-solaire/obligation-ombriere-parking/)).
- **Scale and compliance.**
  - About 11,000 car parks of 10,000 m² or more
    ([Les Énergies Renouvelables](https://www.les-energies-renouvelables.eu/article/actualites/energies/pourquoi-les-ombrieres-solaires-de-parking-nont-toujours-pas-ete-installees-malgre-lecheance-depassee-1121/)).
  - The retail federation FCD cites 21,000 stores and malls with about 70 million m² of parking
    ([AFP via Connaissance des Énergies](https://www.connaissancedesenergies.org/afp/ombrieres-photovoltaiques-sur-les-parkings-la-grande-distribution-demande-une-reglementation-realiste-260702)).
  - Idex, an energy-services firm, estimates that only 10–15% of large car parks were equipped or in
    progress at the deadline
    ([Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/ombrieres-photovoltaiques-sur-les-parkings-la-grande-distribution-temporise-2146151)).

#### 1.8 Mandatory energy audit and ISO 50001 (EU Energy Efficiency Directive via loi DDADUE 2025-391) [LAW]
- The trigger is now consumption, measured per SIREN (legal entity), no longer company size.
- Above 2.75 GWh a year (3-year average), an audit is due every 4 years, the first by **2026-10-11**.
- Above 23.6 GWh a year, an ISO 50001 energy management system is required by **2027-10-11**.
- Firms between the two thresholds that adopt ISO 50001 voluntarily are exempt from the audit.
- Sources: [Diag Tertiaire](https://diag-tertiaire.fr/blog/loi-ddadue-audit-energetique-2026);
  [Mint Énergie](https://blog.mint-energie.com/blog/audit-energetique-ddadue);
  [R3](https://www.r3.fr/ressources/loi-ddadue-guide-complet-entreprises).

#### 1.9 Digital accessibility (European Accessibility Act transposition, RGAA, art. 47) [LAW]
- **Regulators.**
  - DGCCRF, the consumer-protection agency, has had inspection and sanction powers under the European
    Accessibility Act regime since 2025-06-28. A 2026 probe of online shops is reported.
  - Arcom, the media regulator, enforces art. 47.
  - Sources: [AccessiWay on DGCCRF](https://www.accessiway.com/fr/blog/dgccrf-accessibilite-numerique);
    [Axio](https://axio-formation.com/blog/accessibilite-numerique-e-commerce/).
- **First Arcom enforcement.**
  - Arcom's first formal notice went to the tax administration over impots.gouv.fr, dated 2026-06-24
    and published 2026-07-06.
  - It gives 9 months to fix technical defects and 2 months to fix documentation.
  - Arcom aims for several thousand checks a year using automated tools.
  - Source: [AccessiWay on Arcom](https://www.accessiway.com/fr/blog/arcom-mise-en-demeure-impots).
- **Penalties (1src, web agency):** up to €50,000 a year per non-compliant site, plus €25,000 for a
  missing accessibility statement. For private firms above €250M revenue, Arcom's reach is limited to

#### 1.10 Cash-register software certification (NF525) [LAW, relaxed]

#### 1.12 Furnished tourist rentals (meublés de tourisme) [LAW]

#### 1.14 Payroll and social declarations (DSN) [LAW, partial picture]

#### Cross-cutting inferences
- **Dates that held:**

  - NIS2;

  Pattern: France complies late with directives and softens enforcement of its own reforms rather than

- **Election risk.** The French presidential and legislative calendar of 2027 makes any bill "examined

- **The real e-invoicing gap.** The rush in the market is probably the September 2027 issuing wave, not

  actors in scope, but receiving matters only when a supplier must issue.

## 2. EU: deadlines for 2026–2028 and their current status after the omnibus packages

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 omnibus packages postponed obligations that fall mainly on large companies:
- **AI Act** high-risk rules to 2 December 2027 and 2 August 2028;
- **CSRD** (sustainability reporting) narrowed to firms with more than 1,000 employees and €450M
  turnover, with first reports in 2028;
- **CSDDD** (supply-chain due diligence) to July 2029;
- **EUDR** (deforestation) to December 2026 and June 2027;
- **battery due diligence** to August 2027;
- **ETS2** (the second emissions-trading system) to 2028.

Product and market rules mostly applied on time in 2026:
- CBAM's definitive period on 1 January;
- the €3 parcel duty on 1 July;
- the ESPR destruction ban on 19 July;
- PPWR on 12 August;
- CRA reporting on 11 September;
- Data Act art. 3 on 12 September;
- ECGT on 27 September.

Directives that need national law are late almost everywhere (pay transparency, ECGT, right to repair,
EPBD, the Product Liability Directive in progress). The digital omnibus on the GDPR, cookies and the
Data Act is still a proposal.

#### 2.1 AI Act and the Digital Omnibus on AI [LAW, last change 2026-07-24]
- **Procedure:**
  - proposal: 2025-11-19;
  - provisional agreement: 2026-05-07;
  - Parliament vote: 2026-06-16 (423 for, 57 against, 174 abstentions);
  - Council adoption: 2026-06-29;
  - published in the Official Journal on **2026-07-24** as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.

  Source: [EP Legislative Train](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/package-digital-package/file-digital-omnibus-on-ai) (off.).
- **New dates.**
  - Stand-alone high-risk systems: **2027-12-02**.
  - High-risk AI embedded in products: **2028-08-02**.
  - Marking of AI-generated content: **2026-12-02**, but only for systems placed on the market before
    2026-08-02.
  - The deal uses fixed dates. It does not tie them to the availability of standards (same source).
- **Other changes:**
  - a new ban on AI that generates non-consensual intimate content;
  - a lighter registration duty restored for AI systems that are not high-risk;
  - AI-enabled machinery moved under the Machinery Regulation;
  - medium-sized companies keep simplified documentation.

  Same source. Gibson Dunn's summary of the deal:
  [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/).
- **Penalties (art. 99):**
  - prohibited practices: €35M or 7% of turnover;
  - other obligations, including art. 50 transparency: €15M or 3%;
  - incorrect information to authorities: €7.5M or 1%;
  - SMEs pay the *lower* of the two amounts.

  Source: [AI Act art. 99](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/99/).

#### 2.2 CSRD and CSDDD after Omnibus I [LAW, last change 2026-02-26]
- **The act.** Directive (EU) 2026/470 was published in the Official Journal on 2026-02-26 and took
  effect on 2026-03-18 ([Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-sustainability-omnibus-published-in-the-official-journal);
  [Covington](https://www.insideenergyandenvironment.com/2026/02/eu-csddd-csrd-omnibus-published-in-official-journal-transposition-delegated-acts-and-guidelines-are-next/)).
- **CSRD scope:**
  - more than 1,000 employees **and** more than €450M turnover;
  - EU groups report first on financial years starting in 2027, so first reports in 2028;
  - non-EU parents: more than €450M EU turnover, plus an EU subsidiary or branch above €200M, from
    FY2028.
- **CSDDD scope:**
  - more than 5,000 employees and €1.5bn turnover;
  - applies from **2029-07-26**;
  - transposition due by 2027-03-19.
- Sources for both: [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-sustainability-omnibus-published-in-the-official-journal);
  [Covington](https://www.insideenergyandenvironment.com/2026/02/eu-csddd-csrd-omnibus-published-in-official-journal-transposition-delegated-acts-and-guidelines-are-next/).
- **Effect for SMBs:** demand for CSRD from mid-sized companies has largely disappeared, apart from
  value-chain data requests.

#### 2.3 EUDR (deforestation regulation) [LAW, last change 2025-12 (Reg. 2025/2650) and 2026-05-04 (review)]
- **Dates:**
  - **2026-12-30** for large and medium operators, and for micro and small timber firms;
  - **2027-06-30** for other micro and small firms.
- **Simplification review of 2026-05-04 (COM(2026) 191).**
  - It did **not** reopen the regulation.
  - It brought guidance, a draft delegated act on scope (soluble coffee and some palm derivatives
    added; leather and retreaded tyres removed) and upgrades to the information system.
  - Sources: [EUR-Lex COM(2026)191](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A52026DC0191) (off.);
    [Arendt](https://www.arendt.com/news-insights/news/eudr-simplification-review-published-what-the-2026-package-means-for-in-scope-companies/);
    [Integritynext](https://www.integritynext.com/resources/blog/article/eudr-review-out-now-minor-simplifications-same-deadline).
- **Who files what.**
  - The first firm to place goods on the EU market still files due-diligence statements with
    geolocation data.
  - Downstream firms and traders of micro, small or medium size mostly just collect information.
  - Small producers in low-risk countries may file a one-off simplified declaration.
  - Sources: [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0237-eu-deforestation-regulation-application-postponed-to-30-december-2026);
    [Coolset](https://www.coolset.com/nl/academy/eudr-reporting-guide-for-traders).
- **Cost.** The Commission estimates the 2025 changes plus this package cut annual compliance costs by
  about 75% ([Arendt](https://www.arendt.com/news-insights/news/eudr-simplification-review-published-what-the-2026-package-means-for-in-scope-companies/)).

#### 2.4 Cyber Resilience Act [LAW, last change 2026-09-11]
- **Reporting duty, in force since 2026-09-11:**
  - manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents;
  - deadlines: an early warning within **24 h**, a notification within 72 h, and a final report within
    14 days of a fix (vulnerabilities) or 1 month (incidents);
  - reports go through ENISA's Single Reporting Platform, live since 2026-09-11;
  - open-source stewards must report from 2027-12-11.

  Source: [EC CRA reporting](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting) (off.).
- **Who and what.**
  - The duty also covers products already on the market
    ([ComplyCRA](https://complycra.eu/what-are-the-cra-obligations-starting-september-11-2026/);
    [Finite State](https://finitestate.io/blog/cyber-resilience-act-fines-and-penalties)).
  - Law firms tell clients to be ready for the "24-hour clock"
    ([Crowell](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/its-live-the-cyber-resilience-act-reporting-is-mandatory-as-of-today-11-september-2026)).
  - Full application on 2027-12-11
    ([CEMA](https://www.cema-agri.org/publications/18-news-publications/1118-joint-industry-call-on-cybersecurity-and-the-machinery-regulation)).
- **Penalties.**
  - Breaches of Annex I and arts. 13–14 (including reporting): up to €15M or 2.5% of worldwide turnover.
  - Other duties: €10M or 2%.
  - Misinformation: €5M or 1%.
  - Source: [Prighter, art. 64 text](https://prighter.com/resources/laws/cyber-resilience-act/articles/article-64).
- **Free EU-funded SME toolkits already exist:**
  - OCCTET (Eclipse Foundation): readiness questionnaire, SBOM (software bill of materials), known
    vulnerabilities ([Eclipse](https://newsroom.eclipse.org/node/44033));
  - CRACY ([Resillion](https://www.resillion.com/latest-news/introducing-cracy-simplifying-cyber-resilience-compliance));
  - OSCRAT ([BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/created-bg/1202158-bulgarian-innovation-hub-joins-european-consortium-launching-cyber-resilience-pl)).
- **Gap the toolkits show.** Early tests found SMEs "struggle to prove how software risk is managed"
  ([Eclipse](https://newsroom.eclipse.org/node/44033)).

#### 2.5 PPWR (packaging and packaging waste regulation) [LAW, in force since 2026-08-12]
- **Applies on time.** The Commission confirmed 12 August and issued an enforcement FAQ on 2026-08-03.

  ([Lappa](https://lappa.org/blog/ppwr/what-you-need-to-know-about-ppwr-2026-postponement/);

  [Packa fact-check](https://contenthub.packa.com/ppwr-postponement-fact-check-2026)).

  - The remaining duties phase in from 2028 to 2040
    ([Ecomundo](https://ecomundo.eu/en/blog/ppwr-regulation-packaging-obligations-companies)).

  ([IHK Halle](https://www.ihk.de/halle/produktmarken/umwelt-und-energie/verpackungen/ppwr-neue-pflichten-fuer-unternehmen-ab-august-2026-7086270)).

    ([Freyr](https://www.freyrsolutions.com/blog/ppwr-substances-of-concern-what-changes-from-august-2026)).
  - Brand owners who set the packaging specification generally count as "manufacturers"
    ([Chemradar](https://www.chemradar.com/en/service/detail/fifwnzue9z40)).
- **Pending changes.**
  - Environmental omnibus COM(2025) 982 of 2025-12-10 would touch art. 45(3) (authorised
    representatives). It is still pending.
  - Implementing acts are late: the producer-register format was due February 2026 and the label
    format August 2026.
  - Sources: [Lappa](https://lappa.org/blog/ppwr/what-you-need-to-know-about-ppwr-2026-postponement/);
    [EPR Representative](https://eprrepresentative.com/blog/eu-omnibus-epr-authorised-representative-france).
  - Dates could still move with follow-on law
    ([Latham via JD Supra](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/european-packaging-and-packaging-waste-9590478/)).

- **The omnibus pattern.** The omnibus moved *large-company reporting and governance* duties
  (AI high-risk, CSRD, CSDDD, battery due diligence) and *deadlines tied to missing infrastructure*
  (EUDR's information system, AI standards). It did not move *product-safety and market rules already
  in application* (CRA reporting, PPWR, ESPR ban, €3 duty). A solo founder should bet on the second
  kind.
- **Late transpositions.** When a directive is late (ECGT, pay transparency, right to repair), demand
  comes in waves per member state. The first-moving states can serve as test markets.

## 3. Platforms: deprecations, forced migrations, price increases and switching waves (2025–2027)

### Takeaway
Most of the big platform cutoffs of 2026 are **already past**:
- Shopify Scripts on 30 June, and the old checkout pages on 26 August for non-Plus stores;
- Google's Content API for Shopping on 18 August;
- Customer Match uploads through the Ads API on 1 April;
- Meta's 7-day and 28-day view attribution windows on 12 January;
- WhatsApp's ban on general-purpose AI chatbots in January (the EU ordered it reversed in June);
- Salesforce Workflow Rules and Process Builder on 31 December 2025;
- the HCP Terraform legacy free plan on 31 March 2026.

Still ahead:
- Microsoft: legacy SMTP AUTH turned off by default at the end of December 2026; Exchange Web
  Services fully blocked on 1 April 2027; Windows Server 2016 on 12 January 2027; Windows 10 ESU
  extended to 12 October 2027.
- TLS certificate lifetime cut to 100 days on 15 March 2027.
- Android developer verification going worldwide in 2027.
- Atlassian Data Center expansions ending on 30 March 2028.

Switching waves with evidence of demand: VMware under Broadcom (by far the largest), the Atlassian
Data Center end of life, Microsoft Publisher, Skype, Workplace from Meta, Bitnami images and HCP
Terraform.

## 4. Which five or six entries have the best ratio of forced demand to vendor crowding for a solo bootstrapper

### Takeaway
Six entries combine a hard date, legal or technical penalties, buyers that can be found and served,
and no crowd at the specific job. Best first:
1. EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) reporting and vulnerability handling for small manufacturers.
2. A green-claims scanner for B2C web shops under the ECGT directive.
3. Renewal automation for TLS certificates that cannot use ACME, sold through MSPs (managed IT
   providers).
4. PPWR declaration-of-conformity files for small packaging brands.
5. A loi APER car-park diagnostic and exemption service in France.
6. EUDR due-diligence statements done for micro and small first importers.

Three rank lower:
- the French e-invoicing wave, which is huge but crowded and only reachable through a vertical wedge;
- the Microsoft 365 EWS / SMTP AUTH cutover, which is dated but quickly absorbed by mail relays and
  MSPs;
- meal-voucher reconciliation for restaurants, a watchlist item until the Senate votes.

### Cited Findings

**1. CRA reporting and vulnerability-handling desk**
- Reporting duties have applied since 2026-09-11, with 24 h, 72 h and 14-day clocks and filing
  through ENISA's platform ([EC](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting)).
- They reportedly cover products already on the market
  ([Finite State](https://finitestate.io/blog/cyber-resilience-act-fines-and-penalties)).
- Full application, including CE marking, on 2027-12-11
  ([CEMA](https://www.cema-agri.org/publications/18-news-publications/1118-joint-industry-call-on-cybersecurity-and-the-machinery-regulation)).
- Fines up to €15M or 2.5% of turnover
  ([Prighter](https://prighter.com/resources/laws/cyber-resilience-act/articles/article-64)).
- What exists: free EU toolkits (OCCTET, CRACY, OSCRAT) that cover assessment and SBOMs
  ([Eclipse](https://newsroom.eclipse.org/node/44033);
  [Resillion](https://www.resillion.com/latest-news/introducing-cracy-simplifying-cyber-resilience-compliance)).
- The weak point they found: SMEs "struggle to prove how software risk is managed"
  ([Eclipse](https://newsroom.eclipse.org/node/44033)).
- EU funding targets compliance tools for micro and small enterprises
  ([NKCS](https://nkcs.bund.de/en/funding-programmes/digital-eccc-2024-deploy-cyber-06-compliancecra)).

**2. ECGT green-claims scanner**
- Applies from 2026-09-27 across the EU. France has not transposed it, and the DGCCRF enforces only
  after transposition ([Village Justice](https://www.village-justice.com/articles/allegations-environnementales-septembre-2026-directive-applique-france-pas,59252.html)).
- The parallel Green Claims Directive is shelved, so the ECGT bans are the binding rule
  ([Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/european-commission-announces-intention-to-withdraw-eu-green-claims-directive-proposal)).
- Visible suppliers are law firms and a few specialist sites
  ([Empcora](https://empcora.com/fr/directive-2024-825);
  [Projet Celsius](https://projetcelsius.com/en/blog/empco-directive-green-claims-guide/);
  [R3](https://www.r3.fr/ressources/directive-ecgt-nouvelles-regles-communication-environnementale)).

**3. Short-lived TLS certificate automation**
- 100-day limit from 2027-03-15 and 47 days from 2029-03-15, with no grace period
  ([SSL.com](https://www.ssl.com/article/ssl-certificate-validity-changes-what-you-need-to-know/);
  [SSLInsights](https://shop.sslinsights.com/blog/ca-browser-forum-47-day-certificate-roadmap/)).

**4. PPWR conformity files for small brands**
- Applies since 2026-08-12 with no grace period
  ([Packa](https://contenthub.packa.com/ppwr-postponement-fact-check-2026)).
- Suppliers must give manufacturers the data that proves conformity
  ([Freyr](https://www.freyrsolutions.com/blog/ppwr-substances-of-concern-what-changes-from-august-2026)).
- Brand owners count as manufacturers
  ([Chemradar](https://www.chemradar.com/en/service/detail/fifwnzue9z40)).
- PFAS limits for food-contact packaging apply now
  ([IHK Halle](https://www.ihk.de/halle/produktmarken/umwelt-und-energie/verpackungen/ppwr-neue-pflichten-fuer-unternehmen-ab-august-2026-7086270)).

**5. Loi APER car-park diagnostic**
- The 1,500–10,000 m² band is due 2028-07-01. Fines are €20,000–40,000 per year per site
  ([Irisolaris](https://irisolaris.com/loi-aper-obligations-ombrieres-photovoltaiques/);
  [SIRENERGIES](https://www.sirenergies.com/en/article/loi-aper-2026-obligations-entreprises)).
- The ≥10,000 m² band missed its date at 10–15% equipped
  ([Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/ombrieres-photovoltaiques-sur-les-parkings-la-grande-distribution-temporise-2146151)).
- Retail alone has 21,000 sites
  ([AFP via Connaissance des Énergies](https://www.connaissancedesenergies.org/afp/ombrieres-photovoltaiques-sur-les-parkings-la-grande-distribution-demande-une-reglementation-realiste-260702)).
- Postponement needs documented commitments, with dated contracts and orders
  ([Ombritech](https://ombritech.com/loi-aper/); [Irisolaris](https://irisolaris.com/loi-aper-obligations-ombrieres-photovoltaiques/)).

**6. EUDR due diligence done for you**
- Micro and small firms apply from 2027-06-30
  ([EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0237-eu-deforestation-regulation-application-postponed-to-30-december-2026)).
- The first firm placing goods on the market still files due-diligence statements with geolocation
  ([Coolset](https://www.coolset.com/nl/academy/eudr-reporting-guide-for-traders)).
- Supplier GPS data "takes months" to collect
  ([osapiens](https://osapiens.com/blog/blog-brightside-of-eudr/)).
- The May 2026 review did not reopen the text
  ([EUR-Lex COM(2026)191](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A52026DC0191)).

**Ranked lower**
- **E-invoicing.** About 10 million actors in scope
  ([AN written question 18851](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q17/17-18851QE.htm)) against
  130–156 approved platforms, some free
  ([ITSocial](https://itsocial.fr/metiers/metiers-actualites/facturation-electronique-la-dgfip-annonce-une-tolerance-au-demarrage-et-ouvre-un-numero-dassistance/);
  [Bourse Inside](https://bourseinside.fr/economie/facturation-electronique-au-1er-septembre-cette-obligation-change-tout-pour-votre-entreprise-voici-les-149-plateformes-agreees/)).
- **Microsoft SMTP AUTH.** Off by default at the end of December 2026, with an admin override
  ([Microsoft](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/exchange/updated-exchange-online-smtp-auth-basic-authentication-deprecation-timeline/4489835)).
- **Meal vouchers.** Senate pending; 2028 dates
  ([LégiSocial](https://www.legisocial.fr/actualites-sociales/8196-tickets-restaurant-changements-prevus-proposition-loi.html)).

#### Why these six beat the obvious big ones
- **French e-invoicing is the largest forced change, but the wrong fight.** About 10 million actors are
  in scope, yet the state approved 130–156 platforms, many with free tiers. A solo founder can only
  win as a *vertical connector* or an *onboarding service for accounting firms*. Pick a vertical
  whose software has no platform link and sell before 1 September 2027.
- **Already gone:**
  - Platform deprecations of 2026 (Shopify, Google, Meta) closed before October 2026. Free community
    tools appeared within weeks.
  - VMware and Atlassian are enterprise markets dominated by large partners.
- **Postponed or shrunk:**
  - CSRD and CSDDD shrank to the largest firms.
  - AI Act high-risk moved to December 2027 and August 2028.
  - NIS2 France has no law yet.
  - French pay transparency moved to 2027–2028.
  - All of them are crowded with vendors who arrived before the delays.
- **The common profile of the six:**
  - The rule *already applies*, or applies by a hard date in 2027–2028 that survived the omnibus round.
  - The work is a recurring workflow (report, scan, renew, document), not a one-off.
  - Buyers cluster in reachable channels: app stores, MSP communities, trade bodies, open map data.
  - Incumbents focus on large accounts or on the next step (installation, enterprise certificate
    management, EPR registration), leaving the small-company workflow thin.

#### Watchlist (re-check monthly)
- **Meal vouchers.** Senate vote. If the bill passes, the yearly commission statements and the 2028 end
  of paper create a reconciliation need for about 235,000 merchants.
- **Microsoft SMTP AUTH hard date** (to be published in H2 2027) and the **EWS final block** on
  1 April 2027.
- **Pay transparency.** The first transposing member states could be test markets for a job-ad
  pay-range checker.
- **NIS2 France.** Watch for the Assemblée scheduling the bill.
- **EUDR.** Watch for adoption of the delegated act on scope.
- **The digital omnibus on GDPR and cookies.** If it is adopted, consent banners change. Consent-tool
  vendors will absorb most of it.

### Gaps
- No count of businesses affected was found for the CRA, ECGT, PPWR or EUDR micro operators. The
  "number of buyers" scores are therefore qualitative.
- Vendor crowding is judged from who shows up in search results and from named vendors, not from a
  systematic count. A competitor sweep (app-store search, Crunchbase, "alternative to" queries) is
  needed before committing to any pick.
- No pricing data was collected for incumbents in the six picks, so willingness to pay is unmeasured.
  That should be the next research step: `unknown — measure by pricing pages and a smoke test`.
- The penalty figures behind picks 1 and 5 come from secondary reproductions (CRA art. 64) or trade
  press (loi APER). Check them on EUR-Lex and Légifrance before using them in marketing copy.
