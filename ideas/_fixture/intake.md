# Intake — Fixture idea (dry runs only)

## Pitch (verbatim)

An online booking page for independent dog groomers in France. Clients pick a free slot and get a reminder the day before, and a small deposit discourages no-shows. The groomer shares one link on her social profiles and stops answering booking messages in the evening.

It costs 19 EUR a month. This pitch is invented: it exists so that every skill and tool can be dry-run against a complete idea without spending anything.

## Intake answers

- Geo: FR. Language: fr.
- Buyer: independent dog groomers, who sign for themselves.
- Value per customer: 228 EUR (12 months at 19 EUR).
- Cap: 300 EUR cash. Founder rate: 100 EUR/h.
